Capabilities

Every module, one source of truth.

When finance, people and operations share one data model, numbers stop drifting apart. We design each module so it reinforces the others.

Financial management

One ledger. Close faster, report with confidence.

We implement and extend financials end-to-end on the same data model as your people and operations, so finance never has to reconcile against another system's version of the truth.

  • General Ledger & closeChart of accounts, consolidation, intercompany
  • Payables & receivablesInvoice automation, collections, cash application
  • Projects & expensesProject costing, billing and expense policy
  • Revenue & assetsRevenue recognition and fixed asset lifecycle
Human capital management

An HR system that mirrors how you really work.

From core HR and recruiting to compensation, absence and performance, we configure, migrate, test and take HCM live — across countries and complex organisation structures.

  • Core HR & organisationPositions, hierarchies, security and self-service
  • Recruiting & onboardingCandidate pipeline, offers and first-day readiness
  • Compensation & benefitsSalary, bonus and equity plans; open enrolment
  • Absence, time & talentLeave rules, shifts, goals, reviews and succession
Payroll

No first-cycle surprises. That's the goal.

Whether you run payroll natively in your ERP or keep a specialist provider for country coverage, we design, connect and parallel-test until results match to the cent.

  • Native payroll designEarnings, deductions, tax setup and pay groups
  • Third-party providersADP, Dayforce, SAP payroll and regional providers
  • Parallel runsMultiple cycles reconciled to legacy before go-live
  • GL & retro handlingPay results and journal entries posted back, audited
Supply chain & operations

From requisition to receipt, fully traceable.

We connect procurement, inventory and fulfilment to the ledger so spend is controlled, stock is visible and every transaction has an audit trail.

  • Procure-to-payRequisitions, approvals, POs and three-way match
  • Inventory & warehousingMulti-site stock, valuation and replenishment
  • Order managementQuote-to-cash flows connected to billing
  • Supplier managementOnboarding, contracts and performance tracking
Analytics & insight

We blend ERP data with CRM, data warehouse and legacy sources under one governed permission model, then deliver dashboards in weeks — not quarters.

  • Workforce dashboardsHeadcount, attrition and pay-equity insight
  • Cost & headcount modelsPlans and forecasts tied to live actuals
  • Real-time scorecardsKPIs that refresh with every transaction
  • Governed data accessRow- and column-level security across sources
