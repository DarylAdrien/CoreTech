About CoreTech

We help organisations get the core right.

Finance, people and operations all depend on the ERP at the centre of the business. CoreTech exists to make that core dependable — and to keep it that way.

Too many ERP programmes end at go-live with a system that technically works but nobody fully trusts. We started CoreTech to close that gap.

Our consultants have spent more than a decade implementing and supporting enterprise ERP and HCM platforms — across finance, HR, payroll and analytics, for organisations operating in multiple countries.

Along the way we learned that the best outcomes come from treating ERP as engineering: clear designs, repeatable builds, automated reconciliation and an audit trail for every change. That discipline now runs through every engagement we take on.

We're deliberately focused. We'd rather be the partner who knows your configuration inside out than the one with the longest list of logos.

Go-live isn't the finish line. Success is the hundredth payroll cycle running as smoothly as the first.
— The CoreTech delivery principle
